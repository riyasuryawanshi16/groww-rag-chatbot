[QUERY 1: ELSS Tax Saving & Lock-in]
User: What is the lock-in period and tax benefit of an ELSS fund?
Response:
Equity Linked Savings Schemes (ELSS) are diversified equity mutual funds that qualify for tax deduction up to Rs 1.5 lakh under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act. ELSS has a statutory lock-in period of 3 years from the date of each allotment, which is the shortest lock-in among all Section 80C tax-saving instruments. At least 80% of total assets in an ELSS fund must be invested in equity and equity-related instruments.

[QUERY 2: Cut-off Timings for NAV Allocation]
User: What are the cut-off timings for mutual fund purchases to get same-day NAV?
Response:
Under SEBI guidelines, the cut-off time for same-day NAV allocation on subscription and redemption for equity and debt schemes is 3:00 PM. Applications and funds received by the fund house before 3:00 PM receive the same day's closing NAV, while orders submitted after 3:00 PM get the next business day's NAV. For liquid and overnight funds, the purchase cut-off time is 1:30 PM with realization of funds before cut-off.

[QUERY 3: Large-Cap vs Flexi-Cap Definition]
User: What is the difference between Large-Cap and Flexi-Cap mutual funds under SEBI rules?
Response:
Large-cap mutual funds are open-ended equity schemes that invest a minimum of 80% of their total assets in equity shares of top 100 companies by full market capitalization. Flexi-cap mutual funds are open-ended equity schemes that mandate investing at least 65% of total assets in equity and equity-related securities across all market capitalizations. Unlike multi-cap funds which have mandatory minimum allocations in large, mid, and small-caps, flexi-cap fund managers have complete discretion to dynamically adjust allocations across market segments based on market opportunities.

[QUERY 4: Expense Ratio & Direct Plans]
User: What is the Expense Ratio and why is it lower for Direct plans?
Response:
The Expense Ratio is the annual percentage of a scheme's average net assets charged by the AMC to cover fund management, custodian, audit, and administrative costs. SEBI caps the maximum Total Expense Ratio (TER) based on the asset size of the fund, with lower limits as assets grow. Direct plans feature a significantly lower expense ratio than regular plans because no distributor commissions are deducted.

[Source: Expense Ratio in Mutual Funds](https://groww.in/p/mutual-funds/expense-ratio)

[QUERY 5: SBI Bluechip Fund Scheme Mandate]
User: What is the investment mandate and benchmark of SBI Bluechip Fund?
Response:
SBI Bluechip Fund is an open-ended large-cap equity scheme with the primary investment objective of providing investors with long-term capital growth from an active portfolio of large-cap equity shares. The fund invests at least 80% of its net assets in equity shares of the top 100 companies by market capitalization, benchmarked against S&P BSE 100 TRI. The scheme has an exit load of 1% for redemptions within 1 year and 0% thereafter.

[Source: SBI Bluechip Fund Direct Growth](https://groww.in/mutual-funds/sbi-large-cap-direct-plan-growth)
